Add-ons

These Add-ons will be available for Firefox, Chrome and Edge at minimum.

  1. 10ten Japanese Reader

    2. Simply hover over Japanese text and a popup displays corresponding dictionary definitions. Available for Firefox, Chrome, Edge, Safari.

  2. Copyfish 🐟 Free OCR Software

    3. Copy, paste and translate text from any image, video or PDF. Available for Firefox, Chrome, Edge

  3. Dualsub

    4. Display dual subtitles on video websites, mainly YouTube. Available for Firefox, Chrome, Edge

  4. YouTube Dual Subtitles

    5. Another one. Has some more quality of life features. Available for Firefox, Chrome, Edge.

Useful links (legal)

Useful links for learning Japanese. Since we are all law-abiding citizens, only legal ones will be presented.

  1. renshuu

    2. Has a variety of things, but in my opinion, its dictionary and kanji search is especially useful. The entries are often accompanied mnemonics, which are user-provided and quite memorable.

  2. jisho dictionary

    3. Dictionary for... well, looking up words and kanji. Useful for identifying unknown kanji you encounter on your journey if you can identify radicals or at least draw the kanji.

  3. Tae Kim's Guide to Learning Japanese

    4. Approachable guide for learning Japanese. The grammar guide is especially popular.

  4. Kanji Damage

    5. Learn 1,700 kanji with vulgar jokes. The kanjis are ordered by similarity as the didactical approach.

  5. Kanji Portraits

    6. Origins, Radicals and Stories of 2000+ most used Kanji are discussed. The etymology is based on views from scholars in the field.

  6. Online Japanese Accent Dictionary (OJAD)

    7. Pitch Accents of verbs and adjectives and their conjugations.

  7. ttsu Ebook Reader

    8. The reader ttsu app is simply an online e-book reader. You upload epubs/e-books you yourself own to be able to read. Why would you use it? Because this online e-book reader supports dictionary extensions like Yomitan or 10ten (mentioned above in the add-ons section). For legal reasons: how you get those epubs is none of my concerns.

  8. animelon

    9. Ok, not exactly the most legal, but it allows you to watch a selection of anime exactly to your learning needs. Allows dual-subtitles, furigana, automatic pauses and more.
