Is your zincate a solid or a solution?

zincate solution:

Do a reverse titration. It is assumed, that your zincate solution (analyte) is added to your iodine stock solution in THF (titrant).

  1. Input mass of iodine I2
  2. Moles of iodine will be calculated
  3. Input used volume of zincate
  4. Input how many equivalents of iodine are used up per zincate
  5. Concentration of zincate will be calculated
  6. Extra: input total volume of zincate
  7. Extra: calculate total moles of zincate inside solution
