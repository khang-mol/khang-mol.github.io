Grignard (or lithium organyl) titration calculator

For the titration of organometallic reagents (usually Grignard but sometimes lithium organyl reagents), Salicylaldehyde phenylhydrazone (Love's reagent) can be used as an indicator. The original paper by Love and Jones can be found here: J. Org. Chem. 1999, 64, 3755–3756.

Here is the titration procedure itself:
"An accurately weighed sample of 1 (typically between 60.0 and 80.0 mg) is dissolved in 10 mL of freshly distilled THF and stirred at room temperature under nitrogen while the organometallic reagent is added slowly by means of a gastight syringe. A yellow color (the monoanion) forms initially, the end point being indicated by a change in this color to bright golden orange."
